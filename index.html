Privacy Policy – Library Drops

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Library Drops respects your privacy. The app is designed to provide short-form content such as facts, educational information, entertainment, humor, and other messages based on the topics you choose.

Information We Collect

Library Drops may collect and store the following information:

  • Account information provided through your sign-in provider, such as your email address, display name, user identifier, and authentication provider
  • User preferences, including the categories and subcategories you select
  • Saved content, including Favorites and notification History
  • Basic device and app information used to support notifications and app functionality, such as platform, locale, time zone, notification status, app version, and a device registration identifier
  • Purchase and entitlement information needed to determine whether subscriptions or other in-app purchases are active

The information available to Library Drops may depend on the information you choose to provide through your sign-in provider.

Sign-In Services

Library Drops uses Firebase Authentication to manage user accounts. Depending on the platform, users may sign in using:

  • Sign in with Apple on iOS
  • Google Sign-In on Android

Authentication providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

How We Use Information

Information collected by Library Drops is used to:

  • Authenticate and maintain your account
  • Personalize Drops based on the topics you select
  • Save and synchronize your selected categories and subcategories
  • Save your Favorites and notification History
  • Prepare and manage daily notifications
  • Determine subscription and ad-free entitlement status
  • Display and manage advertising in accordance with applicable privacy choices
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app experience

Data Storage

Library Drops uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Firebase Authentication is used to manage account authentication. Cloud Firestore is used to store account information, preferences, Favorites, History, and information needed to support app features.

Some information may also be stored locally on your device to improve performance, preserve preferences, and support app functionality.

Notifications

Library Drops may send one daily notification based on your selected topics. Notifications can be disabled at any time through your device settings.

Information such as your device platform, locale, time zone, notification status, app version, and device registration identifier may be stored to support notification delivery and scheduling.

On platforms where Firebase Cloud Messaging is used, Google may process device registration information required to deliver notifications.

Advertising

Library Drops may display advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Depending on your device, region, privacy choices, and applicable law, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location
  • Device identifiers, including advertising or app-related identifiers when available
  • Advertising information, such as advertisements shown to you
  • Interactions with advertisements and app features
  • Diagnostic information such as crash information
  • App and device performance information

Advertising data may be processed by Google and its advertising partners for purposes such as delivering ads, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, analytics, and improving advertising services.

Privacy and Advertising Choices

Library Drops uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices where required.

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may be presented with options to consent to certain advertising uses of your data, decline consent, opt out of certain sale or sharing of data, or manage your privacy preferences.

When required for your region, Library Drops provides a "Privacy and Cookie Settings" option within the app's Settings screen so that you can review or change available advertising privacy choices.

Your available privacy choices may vary depending on your country, state, device, and applicable law.

Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Library Drops may offer optional subscriptions and one-time support purchases.

On iOS, purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payment information and provides Library Drops with transaction and entitlement information needed to activate or restore purchased features.

Library Drops does not receive your full payment card information from the App Store.

Purchase records may also be retained by Apple according to Apple's own policies and legal requirements.

Third-Party Services

Library Drops uses third-party services to provide authentication, data storage, notifications, advertising, privacy controls, purchases, and other app functionality.

These services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Google Sign-In on Android
  • Sign in with Apple on iOS
  • Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit on iOS

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms, and security practices.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at: Google Privacy Policy

You can learn more about Apple's privacy practices at: Apple Privacy Policy

Data Sharing

Library Drops does not directly sell your personal information to data brokers.

Information may be transmitted to or processed by service providers used to operate Library Drops, including authentication, cloud storage, notification, advertising, privacy-management, and payment services.

Advertising-related information may be processed or shared by Google and its advertising partners as described above. Where required by applicable law, privacy choices are provided so that you can consent to or opt out of certain advertising-related uses or sharing of data.

Data Retention

Account-related information is retained while your Library Drops account remains active or while it is needed to provide the app's features.

Deleting the Library Drops app from your device does not automatically delete information associated with your account that is stored remotely.

Third-party service providers may retain certain information according to their own retention policies and legal obligations.

Account and Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Library Drops account from within the app by opening Settings and selecting Delete Account.

Account deletion removes your Library Drops profile and associated app data controlled by Library Drops, including your selected topics, Favorites, History, and prepared notification information.

Some information controlled independently by third-party providers, such as App Store transaction records, may remain subject to those providers' own retention policies and legal requirements.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Your Choices

You can:

  • Change your selected topics within the app
  • Add or remove Favorites
  • Disable notifications through your device settings
  • Manage available advertising privacy choices when applicable
  • Purchase, restore, or manage supported subscriptions
  • Sign out of your account
  • Delete your account from the Settings screen

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Library Drops adds or changes features, services, or data practices. The latest version will always be available at this page.
