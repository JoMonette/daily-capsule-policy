Privacy Policy – Daily Capsule

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Daily Capsule respects your privacy. This app is designed to provide users with daily content such as facts, inspiration, entertainment, and other short messages based on the topics they choose.

Information We Collect

  • Basic account information, such as your email address, when you sign in with Google
  • User preferences, such as selected categories and subcategories
  • Saved app content, such as history or favorites, if you use those features

How We Use Information

  • To let you sign in to the app
  • To deliver daily content based on your selected preferences
  • To save your selected categories, history, and favorites
  • To maintain and improve the app experience

Data Storage

Daily Capsule uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Firebase Authentication is used to manage sign-in. Cloud Firestore is used to store app preferences and saved app content.

Daily Capsule does not sell your personal data. Daily Capsule does not use Firebase Realtime Database to store user data.

Notifications

The app may send daily notifications based on your preferences. You can disable notifications anytime in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

This app uses Firebase and Google Sign-In services provided by Google. These services may process data according to Google's own privacy and security practices.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Daily Capsule account data by contacting us at the email address below. If you delete the app from your device, some account-related data may remain stored in Firebase unless a deletion request is made.
