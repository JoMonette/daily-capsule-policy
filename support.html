Guest Access

Library Drops can be used as a Guest without creating or signing in to a personal account.

Guest users can later choose to connect their Library to an account using Sign in with Apple on iOS or Google Sign-In on Android. Supported Guest data may then be linked or merged with that account.

Account and Guest Data Deletion

Signed-in users can permanently delete their Library Drops account from within the app by opening Settings and selecting Delete Account.

Guest users can permanently remove their Guest profile and associated Library Drops data by opening Settings and selecting Delete Guest Data.
