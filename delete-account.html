Delete Your Account or Guest Data – Library Drops

Library Drops can be used either as a Guest or with a signed-in account. You can permanently remove your Library Drops account or Guest data directly from within the app.

How to Delete a Signed-In Account

  1. Open Library Drops
  2. Open the Settings tab
  3. Select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account

Depending on your sign-in provider, you may be asked to authenticate again before account deletion can be completed.

How to Delete Guest Data

  1. Open Library Drops while using the Guest session
  2. Open the Settings tab
  3. Select "Delete Guest Data"
  4. Confirm that you want to permanently delete the Guest data

What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your Library Drops account or Guest data, Library Drops removes app data associated with that account or Guest session, including:

  • Your Library Drops user or Guest profile
  • Your saved category and subcategory selections
  • Your Favorites
  • Your notification History
  • Prepared notification information associated with the profile
  • Device registration information associated with the profile
  • The associated Firebase Authentication user

Important

Account and Guest data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deleting the Library Drops app from your device does not automatically delete remotely stored Library Drops account or Guest data. To remove that data, use the appropriate deletion option in Settings before removing the app.

If You Cannot Access the App

If you are unable to access Library Drops and need help with account or data deletion, contact:

Email: librarydropsapp@outlook.com

When contacting us, include enough information to identify the Library Drops account you are asking about. Do not send passwords or authentication credentials by email.

Questions

If you have questions about account deletion, Guest data deletion, or your data, contact us at:

Email: librarydropsapp@outlook.com
