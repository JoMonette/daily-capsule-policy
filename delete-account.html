Delete Your Account – Library Drops

Library Drops allows you to permanently delete your account and associated app data directly from within the app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open Library Drops
  2. Sign in to your account
  3. Open the Settings tab
  4. Select "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account

Depending on your sign-in provider, you may be asked to authenticate again before account deletion can be completed.

What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your Library Drops account, the app removes account-related data associated with your Library Drops account, including:

  • Your Library Drops user profile
  • Your saved category and subcategory selections
  • Your Favorites
  • Your notification History
  • Prepared notification information associated with your account
  • Device registration information associated with your account
  • Your Firebase Authentication account

Important

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deleting the Library Drops app from your device does not automatically delete your Library Drops account or remotely stored account data. To permanently remove your account, use the Delete Account option in Settings.
