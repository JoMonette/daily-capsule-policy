Delete Your Account – Daily Capsule

If you would like to delete your account and associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to: dailycapsule@outlook.com
  2. Include your account email address
  3. Write "Delete my account" in the subject

What Data Is Deleted

Upon request, we will delete:

  • Your account information
  • Your saved preferences
  • Your history and favorites

Data Retention

Data will be deleted within 7 days of receiving the request.

If you have any questions, contact us at dailycapsule@outlook.com
